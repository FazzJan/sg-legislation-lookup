Quick arrest check

For an offence under another written law, the CPC 2010 First Schedule default turns on the maximum punishment. The Act itself can override that, so check its arrest clause.

How the status is worked out
  1. Penal Code: read straight from the CPC 2010 First Schedule (columns 3–5).
  2. Act-specific power: if the Act has its own arrest clause, it takes precedence over the term default. Example: LCA s.30 makes any offence under that Act arrestable when committed in an officer’s view, even when the maximum is 6 months.
  3. CPC default (offences against other laws): death, or imprisonment of 3 years and upwards → may arrest without warrant, warrant, not bailable. Under 3 years or fine only → shall not arrest without warrant, summons, bailable.
  4. An arrest override doesn’t change bail. Bail still follows the term unless the Act says otherwise (e.g. Vandalism Act s.6).
