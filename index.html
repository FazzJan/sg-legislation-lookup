About data & CPC rules

Penal Code uses the full CPC First Schedule (self-hosted chunks; no third-party runtime dependency). Other Acts are key sections only. If a search is not indexed, the empty state still deep-links to the AGC section page.

Act-specific arrest powers override CPC term defaults. Example: Liquor Control (Supply and Consumption) Act 2015 s.30 \u2014 any police officer may arrest without warrant any person offending, in the officer\u2019s view, against any provision of that Act. So LCA offences with max imprisonment under 3 years (e.g. s.14(2), max 6 months) are still arrestable. Same idea: CMA s.19 (reasonably suspected), POHA s.18 (in view; s.18(2) also covers suspected s.10(1) in prescribed circumstances), MOA s.40 (in view), Vandalism Act s.6 (all offences arrestable and non-bailable), Public Order Act s.40 (in view, any provision), Misuse of Drugs Act s.25 (committed or reasonably suspected \u2014 no in-view limit), Immigration Act s.51(3) (reasonably believes an offence under the Act or regulations \u2014 no in-view limit). Maintenance of Racial Harmony Act 2025 ss.39\u201340: no Act-wide arrest clause found \u2014 CPC term defaults apply (max \u22653 years \u2192 arrestable / not bailable). PC 298 / 298A First Schedule rows were deleted from 15 Sep 2026 by Act 10 of 2025 s.48(1)(b), with savings for pre-commencement offences.

For other laws not listed: max \u22653 years jail \u2192 usually arrestable + not bailable; under 3 years or fine only \u2192 usually not arrestable + bailable, unless the Act says otherwise. Always open the Act on SSO and check for a general power of arrest before applying CPC defaults.
