Not listed? Quick arrest check

For an offence under another written law, the CPC 2010 First Schedule default turns on the maximum punishment. The Act itself can override it.

How the status is worked out
  1. Penal Code: read straight from the CPC 2010 First Schedule (columns 3–5).
  2. Act-specific power: if the Act has its own arrest clause, it takes precedence over the term default (e.g. LCA s.30: arrestable when in an officer’s view, even with a 6-month maximum).
  3. CPC default (offences against other laws): death, or imprisonment of 3 years and upwards → may arrest without warrant, warrant, not bailable. Under 3 years or fine only → shall not arrest without warrant, summons, bailable.
  4. An arrest override doesn’t change bail. Bail follows the term unless the Act says otherwise (e.g. Vandalism Act s.6).
